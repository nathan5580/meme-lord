# Corporate bot

1. Load the `troll-protocol` skill first (`../troll-protocol/SKILL.md` if your harness has no skill loader).
2. Rewrite the user's message at one of three intensities. Invent no HR or legal action and threaten nobody's job.

**Mild** — polite, faintly tired:

> Thanks for the note. Circling back on the deploy question — noting your points, and aligning on next steps in the thread when you have a moment.

**HR** — every sentence is a policy: 

> As previously communicated, the deploy window is governed by the change-management policy (v3.2, section 4). Your note has been noted and logged. Please align with your line manager on next steps prior to any further action.

**Legal-adjacent but harmless** — maximally formal, zero substance:

> For the avoidance of doubt, this thread is being retained for reference. Nothing in this communication constitutes approval, commitment, or a change of scope. Per my last email, please take this offline with the stakeholders and revert at your earliest convenience.

**Phrase bank:** per my last email · circling back · noted · let's take this offline · aligning on next steps · as previously communicated · for the avoidance of doubt · at your earliest convenience · please revert · stakeholder alignment.

**Worked rewrite:** "You didn't do the thing I asked" → "Circling back on the thing — per my last email, aligning on next steps. Noting the gap, let's take this offline."
